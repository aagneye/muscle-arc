Protocol (host's method)

1 Thickness, 3 lines: click the inner edge of the upper (superficial) aponeurosis, then the inner edge of the lower (deep) aponeurosis. Left, middle, right of the muscle. Lines snap vertical; hold Shift for a free line.

2 Fascicles, 3 fibres: start on the deep aponeurosis, click along a clearly visible fascicle (up to 2 bends), end on the superficial aponeurosis. Extend past the image edge into the grey margin if the fibre leaves the frame. Enter or double-click finishes. Prefer fibres needing the least extension, in the middle of the image.

3 Angles, 3 angles: 2 clicks along a fascicle where it meets the deep aponeurosis, then 2 clicks along the deep aponeurosis at that spot.

If the image shows more than two aponeuroses, measure the upper (superficial) muscle.

Quick mode asks for one line of each kind. For calibrating the model, more measured images matter more than more lines per image.
